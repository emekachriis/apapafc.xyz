Apapa Kings FC is a football club from Lagos, born in {{ club.founded }} with one rule written on the wall: Kings never settle.

We play on the pitches Lagos actually has: hard sand, uneven grass, goalposts that have seen things. No fancy facilities, no excuses. Just a squad in orange turning up every matchday to compete for each other and for the badge.

Right now the Kings play friendlies against teams across the city. Every game is a chance to get better, meet new teams and put the name Apapa on the map. If your side wants a game, we're ready.

What never settle means

  1. Show up

    Training, matchday, rain or sun. The shirt is earned by being there.

  2. Work for each other

    Nobody plays for themselves. Win it back, cover the run, lift each other up.

  3. Never satisfied

    Won? Good. Now do it better. Lost? Learn it, own it, come back harder.

  4. Respect the game

    Respect the opponent, the referee and the people who come out to watch.
