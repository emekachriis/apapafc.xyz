A five-goal game at Elemu Field, 721 Festac, ended in a narrow 3–2 home defeat to Catholic Church of the Presentation, the side that also beat the Kings 4–2 in August.

The visitors went ahead on 20 minutes through an Ola own goal, and Lambert doubled their lead seven minutes later. The Kings hit back before the break: Francis pulled one back on 32 minutes and Joseph levelled on 40. Victor's goal on 51 minutes proved the winner.

**Apapa Kings FC 2–3 Catholic Church of the Presentation**

- **Kings:** Francis 32', Joseph 40'
- **Catholic Church of the Presentation:** Ola (own goal) 20', Lambert 27', Victor 51'

**Starting lineup:** Amaechi, Benjamin, Chris, Diamond, Ola, Samuel, Joseph, Francis, Wisdom

**Subs:** Bobo, Alakhumen, Ifeanyi, Praise, Great, Pascal, Buchi, Goodness, Wale, David
