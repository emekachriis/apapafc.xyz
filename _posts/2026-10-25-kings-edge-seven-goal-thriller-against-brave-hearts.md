Apapa Kings FC beat Brave Hearts 4–3 in a seven-goal friendly at Signal Barracks Pitch, Mile 2, the Kings' second win over Brave Hearts after June's 3–2.

Joseph opened the scoring after five minutes, and although Hazard equalised on 10, the Kings took control in a ten-minute spell before the break: Aboy made it 2–1 on 25 minutes, Buchi added a third on 30 and Aboy grabbed his second on 35. Brave Hearts fought back after half-time through Alfa (56') and Jnr Messi (72'), but the Kings held on.

**Apapa Kings FC 4–3 Brave Hearts**

- **Kings:** Joseph 5', Aboy 25', Buchi 30', Aboy 35'
- **Brave Hearts:** Hazard 10', Alfa 56', Jnr Messi 72'

Kings Never Settle.
