Honours even at Oluwa Primary School, Tolu Complex, where Apapa Kings FC and Tolu FC drew 2–2. It's the Kings' first draw.

Chris put the Kings in front on 15 minutes before Bright levelled for Tolu FC on the half-hour. David restored the lead on the hour, but Victor's 85th-minute equaliser meant the points were shared.

**Tolu FC 2–2 Apapa Kings FC**

- **Kings:** Chris 15', David 60'
- **Tolu FC:** Bright 30', Victor 85'

**Starting lineup:** Amaechi, Praise, Benjamin, Chibuzor, Adeleke, Ola, Divine, Joseph, Wisdom

**Subs:** Charles, Chris, David, Ifeanyi

Kings Never Settle.
